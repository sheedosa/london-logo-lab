# The London Logo Lab website — guidance for this repo

Static React site (Vite + Tailwind) on GitHub Pages for the I&A group's embroidery and print workshop. Confirmed by the client: services (embroidery, screen printing, DTF printing, vinyl, workwear supply, uniforms), customers (trades, schools, sports clubs), minimum order 1 item, turnaround within 7 days, priced per item or by quote. The voice is friendly: short sentences, plain words. Not an I&A-branded site: it has its own identity (`docs/BRAND.md`); the group link is a footer line only.

## Rules
1. **Facts come from `src/data/site.ts`; garments from `src/data/garments.json`.** Never invent prices, minimums, setup charges, turnaround, delivery or returns terms, addresses, phone numbers, fabric claims or reviews. Unconfirmed items are `[PLACEHOLDER]` in copy and listed in `SITE.toConfirm`. While `SITE.preview` is true, garments are samples, the pricing formula is labelled as a sample, and quote sending is switched off.
   Contact facts (address, hours, phone, email, legal name) are read through `src/lib/contact.ts`: the header menu, footer and quote form print them only once they are known and show one "to come" line until then; the bracketed placeholders are listed once, on About. No placeholder in a button, a heading or a repeated card line.
2. **Data goes through `src/lib/store.ts`; the quote lives in `src/lib/quote.tsx`.** Components never read the JSON or `localStorage` directly. Every price shown on a page is either `£[PRICE]` or the output of `estimate()` labelled as a sample.
3. **Every page sets `<Seo>`** with title, description, canonical and JSON-LD. Add new routes to `scripts/prerender.mjs`.
4. **The quote builder is the core idea, and it is a job ticket** (`src/components/Ticket.tsx`): garment, colour, quantity, decoration, positions and logo are ruled lines on a paper ticket; the live flat mock-up (`Mock`) sits beside it; the estimate is the ticket's total line below the tear, always labelled as a sample; "ask us for a quote" leads to the send form. Decoration methods (embroidery, screen print, DTF, vinyl) each have their own positions in `garments.json` `methods` and their own sample formula. Any new garment needs a `kind` drawn in `src/components/Garment.tsx` (technical-flat style: ink silhouette, sewn-on pieces filled with the cloth, check it against the real garment) and its positions and methods listed in `garments.json`. The quote state saved on the device must stay valid when the data changes (`sanitise()`), including `done`, the list of ticket lines the visitor has chosen.
5. **Brand (docs/BRAND.md), v5: a light, orange-led workshop.** Paper `#f3eee4` is the ground of every page; Orange `#f4651f` is laid down in whole fields (the Home hero, at most one band or title field on any other page) with Ink type on it, and is the button fill; Ink `#1b1916` is type and 2px borders and is **never a ground** (no ink hero, band, footer or phone bar); Stone `#e6dfd0` is the bench the ticket lies on and the neutral band; Mute `#5f5850` is secondary text on Paper and Stone only (on Orange all text is Ink); Rust `#b54106` is the stamp and the stitched rule on Paper. **Type: Logo Lab owns the heavy slab.** Bitter 900 for the hero, h1 and big numerals, Bitter 800 for h2 and column heads, Bitter 700 for h3 and the ticket's title; sentence case, tracking -0.01em (0 on h3), leading about 1.05 (1.0 on the hero, 1.2 on h3). Archivo 400/500/600 for all text, labels and controls. Archivo Black lives only inside the outlined logo SVGs: never load it or name it in page type. Change `--font-display` / `--font-sans` only; lettering drawn in `Garment.tsx` and the social card in `scripts/brand-assets.mjs` name Bitter too. Sentence case everywhere, buttons included. Corners 3px, 2px ink borders. No gradients as decoration, no pills, no card grids for prose, no stock-photo look: imagery is flat SVG, swatches and labelled photo slots (max two per page). No faces.
6. **Logo files** in `public/img/logo/` are built by `scripts/logo.py`; edit the script, not the SVGs. Rasters and the logo sheet come from `scripts/brand-assets.mjs`.
7. Tap targets ≥ 44px, no horizontal overflow at 375px, body text ≥ 16px, AA contrast with solid tones, visible focus, `aria-pressed` on toggles, one `h1` per page, British English, GBP, no emoji.

## Commands
```bash
npm run dev          # local
npm run build        # typecheck + vite build + prerender (needs Chromium)
npm run build:spa    # build without prerender
```

## Design system (v5)
It must not share a skeleton with Realco (strip, logo/nav/button header, dark hero with the headline in a left column, "heading left, ruled rows right" sections, dark four-column footer). None of those five come back.
- **Type:** one fluid scale in `index.css` (`--step-*`, classes `.t-hero`, `.t-title`, `.t-section`, `.t-head`, `.t-sub`, `.t-quote`, `.t-lede`, `.t-small`, `.t-num`); bare `h1`/`h2`/`h3` already use it. Never set a heading size in a page. `.t-hero` is sized so the Home headline fills the page width on two lines from 1024px (three on a phone); if the headline's words change, re-tune `--step-hero`. `.t-head` is for the head of a narrow column or rail (Services comparison, Garments categories), where `.t-section` would break words. Caps `.label` is for ticket lines, filter names and table rails only, never an eyebrow above a heading. No middle-dot strings, no arrows on links.
- **Header** (`Header` in `Layout.tsx`): no strip. One sticky bar on Paper with a 2px Ink rule: the lockup, then the pages as plain words straight after it (the page you are on underlined in Ink; the quote word always carries the 4px Orange line and reads "Your quote" once one is saved), and the promise from `SITE` as a rust stamp (`.stamp`) at the right. Below `lg`: lockup, an Orange "Quote" button and the menu, which opens a panel with the full nav, the promise, phone, address and hours. The preview notice is in the footer's legal line only.
- **Footer** (`Footer`): Paper, shaped like the bottom stub of the ticket: a line of punched holes across the page (`.tear`), the promise and the quote button on the left, the links as two short lines and the workshop line on the right, then a 2px rule and the family and legal line with the preview notice. No columns, no caps headings, no lockup.
- **Home, in order:** the Orange field with the headline across the full width, the lede beneath it, then the drawing on its own Paper sheet and the working ticket side by side on the orange (`HeroTicket`, `ground="orange"`); the four methods as four centred columns with the close-up large on top (two by two on a phone) and the two supply services as lines under a rule; "Who we make it for" as three compositions (Trades a framed Stone bench, Schools a contained pair on Paper, Sports clubs the one Orange band with the squad lined up); How it works; the garment rail on Stone; the workshop.
- **Sequences are `Steps`** (`src/components/Steps.tsx`, `.steps`): one strip of numbered Orange tiles sewn onto a single Ink thread, across the page from `lg` and down the left edge below it. Used on Home and Services. Do not set a sequence as "heading left, numbered rows right".
- **The ticket is the signature.** The running-stitch border (`Stitch`), the perforated tear and the stitched tick (`StitchTick`) belong to `Ticket` only. The other stitches are `TitleRule`, a short stitched rule under a page title, and the thread in `Steps`. Do not use stitch lines as dividers anywhere else. "1 item minimum, ready within 7 days" is the stamp in the ticket header and comes from `SITE`.
- **Where the ticket appears:** Home hero (a working ticket on the Orange field, lines closed, drawing beside it), `/quote` (all six lines, send form under it) and each garment page (a ticket started for that garment; it stays a local draft until "Quote this garment"). The ticket lies on Stone or Orange, never on Paper: set `ground` so the punched holes show the right colour.
- **Phones:** the mock-up pins under the header as a short strip while the ticket is filled in; `TotalBar` (Paper, 2px Ink rule) shows the sample estimate and the next action whenever the ticket's own stub is off screen.
- **Grounds and focus:** a section on Orange takes `.on-orange` (Ink focus ring with a Paper halo; buttons there are `.btn-ink`, text is Ink, rules are Ink). There is no `.on-ink`.
- **Motion:** tokens `--ease-out`, `--dur-1/2/3`. Motion answers an action (a line opening, a tick stitching in, the drawing changing garment). The one unprompted moment is the hero ticket arriving (`.feed`). Reduced motion switches all of it off.
- **Shared elements:** use `VLink` for journeys into `/quote` and into a garment. The ticket keeps `TICKET_VT`; a garment drawing on a card and on its page share `garmentVT(handle)`; the hero and quote mock-ups share `mock`.
- **Inner pages:** title blocks are Paper with a 2px rule (Services), Stone (Garment, Quote) or an Orange field (Schools & clubs, About). Services compares the four methods on shared rows (`.compare`, subgrid) with a drawn close-up each (`MethodCloseUp`); its Uniforms section is the page's one Orange band. Garments are grouped by category with the category in a left rail; filter state lives in the address (`?in=`, `?takes=`). Facts are set as a `.ledger`.
